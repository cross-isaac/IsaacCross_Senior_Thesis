# 06_Figures_and_Media — Thesis figures, lab photos, and art

## Thesis_Figures/

Figures organized by thesis chapter (folder names reflect an earlier chapter numbering,
kept as-is because the LaTeX source references them):

- `Ch02_Background/` — crystal/dopant artistic rendition (thesis Fig. 2.1).
- `Ch03_Beam Source Design/` — nozzle, capillary loading, heaters, gas handling, He leak
  test, growth-rate heat maps, CAD renders (thesis Ch. 3 figures).
- `Ch04_heater/` — stainless reservoir heater test figures (thesis Sec. 3.5).
- `Ch05_characterization_methods/` — chamber, optics diagrams, periscope, circuit,
  spectra, data-pipeline figures (thesis Ch. 4 figures). `112825/` = photos from the
  Nov 28 2025 Rb-Ar run.
- `ch06_results/` — beam summary plots, capture-efficiency plots, nozzle-overlay figure
  (thesis Ch. 5 figures). `Summary Plots/` is the canonical set (duplicated variants were
  archived to `99_Archive/duplicates`).
- `Ch07_monte_carlo_sim/` — Monte Carlo figures (thesis Ch. 6).
- `Lab Photos/` *(git-ignored — ~1 GB)* — bulk photo/video dump from lab work:
  assembly, capillary loading, chamber builds, bakeouts. Source pool the curated
  chapter figures were picked from.
- Root: `Rb_certification.png`, `Rb_vapor_pressure_curve.png` (thesis Appendix B).

## Lab_Art/

Blender project files and renders of the doped-crystal artistic figure and π/2-pulse
animations (`Pi_2_Pulse*.mp4`) used in talks and the thesis background chapter.
