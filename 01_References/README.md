# 01_References — Papers, theses, textbooks, datasheets

Consolidated reference library (merged from the old `Sources`, `To Organize/NonSpecificSources`,
and `To Organize/Useful Sources` folders; exact duplicates removed).

## Highlights (thesis citation ↔ file)

- **Senaratne et al., RSI 86, 023105 (2015)** — microcapillary array nozzle design the beam
  source is based on → `Microcapillary Design.pdf`
- **Ross & Sonntag, RSI 66, 4409 (1995)** — high-temperature metal atom beam sources
  (alternate divergence formula θ½ ≈ 1.68·ID/L) → `High temperature metal atom beam sources.pdf`
- **Regmi MA thesis (UNR, 2013)** — optical pumping of Rb in solid Ar; oven design reference
  → `Weinstein_Atomic_Oven_Thesis_Regmi.pdf` (excerpt: `Weinstein_Atomic_Oven_Thesis_Regmi_OvenOnly.pdf`)
- **Upadhyay PhD thesis (UNR, 2020)** — spin coherence of alkali atoms in cryogenic matrices;
  source of the Kittel-Abrahams comparison → `2020Upadhyay_PhD_Thesis_SpinCoherence.pdf`
- **Dargyte et al. (Rb:Ne), Upadhyay et al. (Rb:pH2), ACME-II, JILA eEDM** — coherence/eEDM
  benchmarks cited in Ch. 2 and Ch. 6 → individual PDFs and `NSF Research Plan Papers/`
- **Kittel & Abrahams (1953)** — dipolar broadening analytic limit →
  `Dipolar Broadening of Magnetic Resonance Lines in Magnetically Diluted Crystals.pdf`
- **Foot, *Atomic Physics*** and **Moore et al., *Building Scientific Apparatus*** — textbooks
  (Beer-Lambert/saturation; vacuum & conductance formulas). `...Sec 7.6.pdf` is an excerpt.
- **`Our Rb COA 276332-1G-PWMKCS4993.pdf`** — Certificate of Analysis for the actual
  Sigma-Aldrich Rb used (thesis Appendix B).
- **Steck alkali D-line data** → `Rubidium_85_D_Line_Data.pdf` and related.

## Subfolders

- **`NSF Research Plan Papers/`** — curated set collected for the NSF GRFP research plan
  (clocks, matrix-isolation spectroscopy, hybrid quantum systems). Includes a
  `More Focus on Microwave Clock Transitions/` subset.
- **`Equipment_Manuals/`** — Toptica DL Pro / FPI 100, Thorlabs cameras and photodetectors
  (DCC1545M, PDA10A, PDB210A), Rigol MSO5000 scope, SRS RGA, TeachSpin optical pumping
  manual, and the MLV-22 leak valve datasheet (also see `MLV-22 leak valve.pdf` here).

`Compiled Reference List.pdf` / `Compiled References.pdf` are generated bibliography lists.
